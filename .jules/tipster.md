## 2025-02-23 - Career Maiden Insight
**Learning:** The getHorseInsight coordinator explicitly filters out horses with < 3 starts. Insights designed for un-raced or lightly raced horses (like broodmare production without race history, or debut winners) will not trigger unless the coordinator logic is modified.
**Action:** Targeted insights for heavily-raced horses like "Career Maiden" (10+ starts, no wins) fit perfectly within the existing coordinator's constraints and provide highly actionable decisions (retiring or dropping in class).
