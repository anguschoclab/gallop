## 2024-10-18 - Tipster: Earnings per Start
**Learning:** The horse model doesn't store purchase price or ROI data directly. Instead, I had to use `lifetimeEarnings` and `careerStarts` to derive an "Earnings per Start" economic insight, which is a key profitability metric.
**Action:** When asked for economic ROI insights for a horse, use `lifetimeEarnings` and `careerStarts` instead of looking for purchase records.
