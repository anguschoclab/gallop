## 2024-03-24 - Precomputing Race Facade Context in Render Loop
**Learning:** In the high-frequency React component `Track.tsx` mapping loop (~10Hz visualizer), `deriveRunnerConditions` was being called inside the `runners.map` function, while also being called internally by `captureRunnerMoods` on the same runners in the render body. This led to duplicate derivation per runner per frame. Furthermore, `captureRunnerMoods` was unnecessarily re-creating `fieldContext` internally even though it was already available.
**Action:** Hoist the expensive context and condition calculations outside the map loop and pre-compute conditions in a single loop before passing them both to `captureRunnerMoods` and looking them up during the map iteration, massively saving CPU cycles during racing.
