## 2024-05-24 - Expanding Jockey Commentary Templates
**Learning:** Expanding low-count variant pools for frequent events (like jockey moves, mastery, tactics, and apprentice rides) from 12-16 variants to a minimum of 16-22 prevents the game world from feeling repetitive, maintaining immersion for the player.
**Action:** Next time I extend content, I will count the existing templates in a pool and ensure they reach at least ~16-20 variants before adding new systems, prioritizing the pools that are drawn from most frequently.
