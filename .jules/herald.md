## 2024-08-15 - Expanded Flavor Stories for Thin Themes
**Learning:** The `jockeys`, `breeding`, and `weather` themes in `flavorStories.ts` had very few entries (only 2-4 each), leading to repetitive world-building news during long saves.
**Action:** When adding flavor content, prioritize categories with fewer than ~8 variants to ensure the game world feels varied and immersive, avoiding repetition.
