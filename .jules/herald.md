## 2024-05-15 - Expanded Directive News Templates
**Learning:** Found that `generateDirectiveChangeNews` lacked template variety (only 8 variations), which leads to repetitive updates when AI stables shift strategies or fall into financial distress. A content expansion strategy mirroring `generateRivalryEmergenceNews` variety works well here.
**Action:** When working on flavor text systems, count the variants in simple arrays. Anything under ~8 gets stale fast; target at least 14 for news items.
